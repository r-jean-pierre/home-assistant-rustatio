# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Everyone participating is expected to help maintain a respectful, inclusive
environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and code.
- Respect differing experiences, opinions and levels of technical knowledge.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Respect other people's privacy and maintainers' time.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation or discriminatory remarks.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content.
- Publishing personal information, credentials or private communications
  without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other
project spaces associated with this repository. It also applies when someone
explicitly represents the project in an external community.

## Reporting concerns

The project maintainer is [r-jean-pierre](https://github.com/r-jean-pierre).

For a concern that can safely be discussed publicly, contact the maintainer
through an issue in the affected repository. Keep the report factual and
include only the information needed to understand the concern.

For a sensitive report, first request a private reporting channel from the
maintainer. If using a public issue to make that request, include only the
request for private contact, without incident details, names, screenshots or
other sensitive information. Wait for an agreed private channel before
sharing those details.

Never post passwords, access tokens or other secrets.
For security vulnerabilities, follow the repository's published security
policy when available. Otherwise, request a private reporting channel from
the maintainer. Do not disclose vulnerability details in public issues.

## Moderation

The maintainer assesses reports in context and should handle them fairly,
with discretion and respect for everyone involved. Absolute confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed; information should be shared only as needed to address
the concern or meet legal obligations.

Depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior, action may include
a request to stop, a warning, removal of inappropriate content, restrictions
on participation or exclusion from project spaces. Serious incidents may
warrant immediate restrictions. Decisions concern participation in these
project and do not determine access to GitHub as a whole.
